# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-11

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application `Viet Nhat` (`Viet-Nhat`, `the App`).

## 1. Overview

The App is designed to help Vietnamese users living in Japan with practical information such as administrative procedures, labor rights, daily life, taxes, pension, housing, family procedures, and basic Japanese support.

The App is primarily a local-content app. Most guide content is bundled inside the application and can be read without creating an account.

## 2. Data We Collect

We do **not** operate a custom backend that collects user accounts or stores your personal profile on our own server.

Depending on how you use the App, the following data may be stored **on your device**:

- Saved bookmarks
- Checklist progress
- Important dates you add for reminders
- Local app preferences
- AI chat history stored locally on your device
- Your Anthropic API key, if you choose to enter one

## 3. AI Feature

The App includes an optional AI chat feature.

Important points:

- The AI feature is optional.
- To use it, you must manually enter your own Anthropic API key.
- Your API key is stored locally on your device using secure device storage.
- The App does not sell or issue API keys.
- When you use AI chat, your prompt and conversation content are sent directly to Anthropic's API using the API key you provided.
- Your use of the AI feature is also subject to Anthropic's own terms and privacy practices.

If you do not enter an API key, the AI feature will not work.

## 4. Local Notifications

The App can schedule local reminder notifications for important dates that you choose to save, such as card expiry dates or administrative deadlines.

These reminders are generated from the data you enter yourself and are stored on your device.

The App does not currently operate its own remote push notification service.

## 5. Feedback and Contact

If you use the feedback screen, the App opens your email application so you can send feedback to the developer email address.

This means:

- the content you send is handled through your email provider,
- the App itself does not run a separate feedback server for that form.

## 6. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The App does not intentionally require or collect:

- account registration,
- password-based login,
- payment card information,
- government ID uploads to our own backend,
- exact device location,
- contacts,
- microphone recordings,
- camera images for upload,
- advertising identifiers for ad targeting.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party platform or infrastructure components such as:

- Expo libraries
- Apple App Store
- Google Play
- Anthropic API, if you use the AI feature
- your email app, if you send feedback

Those services may process technical data according to their own policies.

## 8. Data Retention

Because most user data is stored locally on your device:

- it remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App, uninstall the App, or remove it manually where supported,
- your AI API key can be removed from the Settings screen,
- your saved dates, bookmarks, and local history can be removed from the App or by uninstalling the App.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is an informational utility app. It is not designed as a children's app and does not intentionally collect personal data from children through a proprietary backend.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable steps at the application level to minimize unnecessary data collection.

However:

- no device is perfectly secure,
- no internet transmission is perfectly secure,
- use of third-party AI services carries third-party risk,
- you are responsible for protecting your own device and your own API keys.

## 11. International Use

The App is intended for users living in Japan, but it may be distributed through international app stores.

If you use the AI feature or email feedback, data may be processed by third-party services outside your country of residence.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, especially if data handling, AI features, or platform permissions change.

The updated version will show a revised `Last updated` date.
